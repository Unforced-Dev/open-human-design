# Line fixing: ▲/▼ data and calculation boundary

The natal engine calculates each planet's gate and line, but `natalengine@1.6.0` does not include fixing rules. Its `src/data/gate-lines.js` contains only `keynote` and `description`; `src/calculators/hd-transits.js` returns transit gate, line and longitude without a fixing state. The app's P2-17 audit records the missing marks.

## Primary rule evidence

[Jovian Archive's explanation of line fixing](https://jovianarchive.com/blogs/chart-interpretations-components/line-fixing-in-human-design-exaltation-detriment-and-juxtaposition) says the rule belongs to a target gate and line, but a matching fixing planet can be in that gate at another line or in the channel's harmonic gate. Its examples include Pluto in 43.2 causing exaltation, Design Sun 55.2 fixed in exaltation by Personality Venus in Gate 39, and Mars in 16.1 fixing the detriment of 16.3 and 16.4. Both poles can be fixed at once, called juxtaposition. Daily transits and another person's chart may also fix a natal line temporarily. [Jovian's MMI product page](https://jovianarchive.com/products/maia-mechanics-imaging-mmi) lists longitude and fixing as separate planetary properties and supports individual, connection and transit charts.

Thus the planet's astronomical longitude yields its gate and line, but not the ▲/▼ mark by itself. A separate planet-by-gate-by-line rule table and the full set of Design and Personality activations are required. For each target activation, compare every planet in its own gate and each channel partner gate with the target's line rule. Merge exalted and detriment contributions; both mean `juxtaposed`. A transit contribution is added only for the queried instant.

## Implemented candidate and provenance

`line-fixing-data.js` adapts the 759 planet/gate/line states from [Christian Reizner's `SharpAstrology.HumanDesign` `HumanDesignUtility.cs`](https://github.com/CReizner/SharpAstrology.HumanDesign/blob/5d95ece57098fd776df7f2362722c1392a6f06b6/Utility/HumanDesignUtility.cs), commit `5d95ece57098fd776df7f2362722c1392a6f06b6` (2026-07-29), under its [MIT license](https://github.com/CReizner/SharpAstrology.HumanDesign/blob/5d95ece57098fd776df7f2362722c1392a6f06b6/LICENSE.md). A local copy is in `docs/licenses/SharpAstrology.HumanDesign-MIT.txt`. This is an open-source candidate, not an officially certified complete ruleset. Its 759 states cover 383 of 384 lines; 54.4 is absent, and the API returns `unknown` for that line. It also returns `unknown` for malformed or incomplete chart input. No Rave I'Ching prose was copied.

`calculateLineFixings(chart, transitActivations?)` in `src/features/transit-timeline/line-fixing.js` is a pure, UI-independent function. `chart.gates.design` and `chart.gates.personality` contain planet-keyed `{ gate, line }` objects. The optional second argument accepts `calculateTransitGates(...).gates` or its planet-keyed equivalent. It returns each natal planet's `natalState`, `transitAdjustedState`, `temporaryChange`, `natalProviders` and `transitProviders`. For example, a sample chart has natal Design Mars 30.3 with `natalState: none`; transit Pluto in Gate 41, the channel partner of Gate 30, changes `transitAdjustedState` to `exalted`. Thus a mark shown beside a natal column during a transit overlay may be temporary.

`calculateTransitLineFixings(chart, transitActivations)` evaluates each transit activation as the target. It reports `transitOnlyState` from the transit planets alone and `combinedState` after adding the chart's Design and Personality planets as fixing providers. The timeline overlay uses `combinedState` in its transit column. These two fields are intentionally separate so a stand-alone transit chart does not silently inherit a person's natal placements.

The regression fixtures exercise 13 transit and 26 natal entries across three snapshots, including simultaneous exaltation and detriment and the difference between transit-only and combined providers. These fixtures verify the implemented calculation paths; they do not independently validate all 759 underlying rule entries.

The [public JSON gist](https://gist.github.com/jdempcy/040d12ae3a97d41a0b93eee401b01486) was not used: it lacks all of Gate 32, has no explicit reuse license, and differs from the MIT candidate in at least six rule entries. No complete official open data license was found during this research. Before showing all marks as canonical, compare a larger set of charts against the official MMI fixing output, especially the conflicting entries, 54.4, lunar nodes as target activations, and transit overlays. The official public explanation gives examples and general mechanics, but does not resolve every edge case of machine-readable chart output.

## Timeline integration

The timeline host adapter supplies `lineFixings` without changing the natal engine or the original four pages. The compact planet rows show the birth target's `transitAdjustedState`; transit rows use `combinedState` in overlay mode and `transitOnlyState` in transit-only mode. Tooltips distinguish natal fixing from a temporary transit change. Unavailable rules show `?` with a verification-pending label. Gate/line values remain independently rendered and natal values stay fixed while scrubbing.
